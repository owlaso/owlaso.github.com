Features

Everything you need for review analysis

Powerful filtering, dual-store comparison, and export tools — built for app developers and ASO professionals.

Dual-Store Reviews

Fetch and compare reviews from Google Play and the App Store side by side. Auto-detect matching apps across stores.

Advanced Filtering

Filter by platform, country, language, star rating, date range, app version, keyword, minimum text length, and developer replies.

ASO Keyword Tracking

Search and analyze keyword rankings with popularity and difficulty scores. Track which apps rank for each keyword.

CSV & JSON Export

Export your filtered review data as CSV or JSON with one click. Ready for spreadsheets, dashboards, or further analysis.

Multi-Country Support

Browse reviews across dozens of countries and storefronts. See how your app performs in different markets worldwide.

Dark Mode

Beautiful dark theme with manual toggle and OS preference detection. Easy on the eyes for long analysis sessions.

How It Works

Up and running in 3 steps

From install to insights in under a minute.

1

Download & Run

Download the installer for Windows, macOS, or Linux and launch OwlASO in seconds — no browser setup required.

2

Search & Select

Search for any app on Google Play or the App Store. Click Use to load its reviews and metadata.

3

Filter & Export

Apply filters, compare stores, track keywords, then export your data as CSV or JSON.
